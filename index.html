Questions

Good to know.

Anything else? Write to team@letdahlia.com.

Does Dahlia pretend to be me?

No. Dahlia opens every call by saying it’s your assistant, that the call is automated and that it’s being transcribed. If anyone asks, it says it’s an AI assistant.

Can it spend my money?

No. If a business asks for a deposit, a payment or a bigger change than you allowed, Dahlia asks you on screen first, and you decide.

Which numbers can it call?

US and Canadian phone numbers. Dahlia won’t call emergency, premium or other restricted numbers. It is not an emergency service; for emergencies, call 911.

What number do businesses see?

A Dahlia number by default. You can verify your own number in the app so businesses can call you back directly.

What happens to my information?

Dahlia uses it only to handle your requests. It’s never sold, used for ads or used to train AI models, and you can delete your account and data anytime. See the Privacy Policy.
